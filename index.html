How this works

Logging drinks. Tap a drink, add halves or pints, give it a 👍 or 👎 and jot a note. Numbers match the festival programme, so you can just tell the bar the number. Stuck for a choice? Tap 🎲.

Your crew. You're in one crew at a time. Everyone in it sees each other's pints, ratings and notes, live. On the Race tab you can tap another crew's name to peek at their race — that doesn't move you, it's just a look. Tasting notes are shared with everyone at the festival, whatever crew they're in, so you get the benefit of the whole room's opinion.

The race. Every half pint moves your glass a furlong. It runs all night and only resets when you tap New session on your own phone. Ratings, notes and trophies always carry over.

Bored? That tab is beer mats for the table — trivia, riddles, phrase origins and groaners. Deal one, read it out, let the table argue, flip it for the answer. The deck won't repeat itself until you've been through the lot.

The bar changes. Only some ciders are out at a time — tap 🍎 Cider bar at the top of the menu and tick what's actually on. Cask run dry? Open it and tap Mark sold out. One person marks it and it flags on everyone's phones, newest change wins.

If the signal drops everything keeps working on your phone and syncs when you're back. Something not listed? Add it at the bottom of the menu.

Small print

Drinks taken from the festival's official lists — the organisers warn these change and not everything is on at once, so treat it as a guide. Units are approximate (half = 284ml, pint = 568ml). Your drinks are stored on your own phone; what you share with your crew travels over the internet, so keep notes printable.
