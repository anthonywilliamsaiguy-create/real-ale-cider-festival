How this works

Tap any drink to log halves and pints, give it a 👍 or 👎, and jot a note. Everything saves to your own phone automatically — no signal needed once the page has loaded. Use Share my tally to send your list to the group chat.

Numbers match the festival programme, so just tell the bar the number. If a cask runs dry, open the drink and Mark sold out — then use the Hide sold out chip to sweep them away. Found something that's not listed? Add it yourself at the bottom of the menu.

The Race tab is the Half-Pint Handicap: every half pint is a furlong. Nothing resets at midnight — drink till 1am if you like; the board only starts fresh when someone taps New day on their own phone (My Night tab).

Getting everyone on the board: each phone keeps its own list, and you swap them through the group chat. Tap Share my tally and send it; when someone else sends theirs, open the Race tab and tap 📋 Paste from chat. Their glass, ratings and notes all land at once. Do it whenever you fancy a look at the standings — a couple of swaps a session is plenty, and it works with no signal.

There's a Trophy Cabinet too (My Night tab): trophies unlock as you explore — perries, stouts, counties, tasting notes, going the distance — and show next to your name on the race. The crew's 👍s crown a Best in Show on the Race tab, and the biggest stinker gets named and shamed.

Crews: met another group who want in? They scan the QR, enter their name and a crew name of their own — they get their own race and your board stays yours. A crew switcher appears on the Race tab, with an All crews view that scores crew against crew. To join a crew that already exists: Race tab → tap that crew's name → tap 🤝 Join. Spelling doesn't matter — "The Lads" and "the lads" count as the same crew, and the crew box suggests existing names as you type. Notes and Best in Show stay within your own crew.
